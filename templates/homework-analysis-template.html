What You Said

Alex flew to Lisbon on Friday. He didn't took a taxi, he took the metro, and at the hotel he rented a room with a view of the river. On the second day the museum was closed and the restaurant was full so they walked to the old town. In the morning, um, he, he found his ticket on the table. He was tired, because he woke up early, so he missed breakfast.

Fix Notes

Grammar
didn't took → didn't take
After didn't the verb stays in its base form: didn't take. Same shape as didn't want, which you already say correctly.
Word choice
rented a room → booked a room
Rent is for a flat and for months. A hotel room is booked. One word, and the whole sentence sounds like a traveller wrote it.
Sentence flow
the museum was closed and the restaurant was full so they walked to the old town → The museum was closed and the restaurant was full. So they walked to the old town.
Four ideas ran on one breath. Cut them at closed, at full, and start the last one with So. Short sentences also give you a place to breathe.
Style & speech
he, he ... um
Restart and thinking sound: both normal in speech. Your ear caught the restart by itself, which is the skill underneath the note.
Punctuation
tired, because → tired because
Russian puts a comma before "потому что"; English runs the two clauses together. Save commas for lists and asides.
