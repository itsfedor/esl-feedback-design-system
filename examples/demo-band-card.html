Quick fixes

Writing · "about 40 per cent" three times → vary: "slightly under half", "just over a third"

Repetition caps the vocabulary score even when every form is correct.

Speaking · "went to city centre" → "went to the city centre"

Articles are the recurring slip; say the fixed phrase out loud until it is automatic.

Reading · choosing "True" when the text is silent → write "NG" unless the exact sentence exists

"Not Given" means the text never says it, not that it is hard to find.
