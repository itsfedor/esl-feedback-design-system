Task 1 · Retell your weekend audio
What they asked: tell the story of your weekend in the past simple, then answer two questions about the trip.

What You Said

Last weekend we goed to Chiang Mai. We taked the night train and arrived on Saturday morning. The hotel was near the river, so we walked to the market, ate street food and watched the sunset over the mountain and then we went back to the hotel. On Sunday my sister wanted to make photos of the temple. It was raining, but um, we, we waited and the sun came out, after lunch.

Fix Notes

Grammar
goed, taked → went, took
Both verbs are irregular, so they change shape instead of taking -ed: went and took. Your regular verb worked fine in the same story: arrived.
Word choice
make photos → take photos
In English, photos are taken. Russian translates this as "make", which is why the pair slips through even at B1.
Sentence flow
...walked to the market, ate street food and watched the sunset and then we went back → We walked to the market and ate street food. We watched the sunset, then went back to the hotel.
One sentence carried four actions. Cut it after food and start again; shorter sentences are easier for the listener and give you places to breathe.
Style & speech
um, we, we ...
A restart and a thinking sound are normal in speech. This one repaired itself in under a second, which is exactly the skill we are training.
Punctuation
came out, after lunch → came out after lunch
No comma before after lunch. The sentence runs without a pause, and commas are for lists and real pauses.

How It Could Sound (Spoken)

More Polished Spoken Version
Last weekend we went to Chiang Mai. We took the night train and arrived on Saturday morning. The hotel was near the river, so we walked to the market and ate street food. We watched the sunset, then went back to the hotel. On Sunday my sister wanted to take photos of the temple. It was raining, but we waited and the sun came out after lunch.
What worked. You kept every action verb in the past from the first sentence, and "the hotel was near the river" shows you can move between was and action verbs inside one story.
Task 2 · Past simple gaps written
What they asked: twelve sentences, put the verb in brackets into the past simple.
Grammar
didn't went → didn't go
After didn't, the verb returns to its base form. You wrote the same pattern correctly in the next sentence, so this one is about speed, not knowledge.
Grammar
buyed → bought
Buy is irregular: bought. Keep it next to brought in your list; the two are easy to mix up.
What worked. Ten of twelve gaps are correct, and all four -ed spelling rules from the lesson landed without a single miss.
